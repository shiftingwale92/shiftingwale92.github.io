Learning by building

Projects

Self-directed study projects in Indian data-protection law. Both are works in progress and every example is fictional - no client, employer or customer data.

01

Indian law notes

Plain-language, primary-source study notes on Indian technology and privacy law. The first note maps the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025: scope, consent and notice, breach intimation, penalties, and the staged commencement timeline from the Gazette notifications - what is in force and what is not, as of the date checked.

Learning project · WIP
02

DPDP compliance workflows

Four preparation workflows - data inventory and mapping, consent notice and log, breach response, and Data Principal rights requests - with illustrative register templates built around a fictional Bengaluru D2C startup. Learning templates, not statutory forms; the Act’s main duties are notified but not yet in force.

Learning project · WIP
